
# MY AI COMPANY
## AI Business Operating System

Version: 1.0
Status: Initial Architecture

## 1. Mission
Build an autonomous AI business operating system with five specialized agents coordinated by one central orchestrator.

## 2. Business Environments

### SI NATURAL
- E-commerce operations
- Shopify products and orders
- Meta advertising
- Customer support
- Inventory and profitability
- Marketing content

### PAYMOB CRM
- Business lead generation
- POS sales pipeline
- Prospect management
- Follow-up automation
- Sales performance reporting

PAYMOB will operate as an independent CRM environment without access to internal company accounts.

## 3. AI Agents

### Agent 01: Research
Research markets, competitors, customers and opportunities.

### Agent 02: Creative
Generate content ideas, ad copy, creative briefs and campaign concepts.

### Agent 03: Media Buyer
Analyze advertising performance, ROAS, CPA and campaign opportunities.

### Agent 04: Sales & CRM
Manage leads, customer conversations, follow-ups and sales pipelines.

### Agent 05: Operations
Monitor orders, inventory, costs, revenue and business reports.

## 4. AI Orchestrator
- Assign tasks to specialized agents.
- Validate agent outputs.
- Manage task handoffs.
- Track execution status.
- Escalate important decisions to the CEO.

## 5. Approval System

CEO approval is mandatory before:
- Publishing advertisements.
- Increasing advertising budgets.
- Changing product prices.
- Sending bulk messages.
- Issuing refunds.
- Performing sensitive business operations.

## 6. Technology Stack

- n8n: Workflow orchestration.
- PostgreSQL: Persistent storage.
- Redis: Task queue when needed.
- AI Models: Agent intelligence.
- Dashboard: CEO control center.
- Official APIs: External integrations.

## 7. Security
- Separate business data.
- Apply least-privilege permissions.
- Protect customer information.
- Never expose API credentials.
- Maintain audit logs.
- Require approval for sensitive actions.

## 8. Operating Principle

Automate repetitive work.
Coordinate specialized intelligence.
Measure business performance.
Escalate important decisions.
Keep the CEO in control.
